# Quipu Systems — imagen v2

Archivo: `assets/archive/quipus/quipu-systems-v2.png`.
Método: herramienta integrada `image_gen`, una generación con cuatro referencias visuales.

## Lectura de las referencias

Se revisaron las ocho imágenes de la carpeta `assets/references/quipus`. Se observan una cuerda principal, cordones colgantes unidos mediante amarres, longitudes y grosores variables, nudos simples y agrupaciones de vueltas en distintas alturas, algunas ramificaciones y agrupaciones de colores. El esquema `quipu2.gif` presenta nudos distribuidos en niveles rotulados como miles, centenas, decenas y unidades. Estas observaciones guían la forma; no se atribuye un significado numérico al render generado.

La nueva imagen mantiene esa estructura y utiliza grafito, plata y turquesa para vincularla con la identidad de Quipu Systems. Es una interpretación contemporánea de marca, no una reconstrucción arqueológica.

## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original static hero artwork for Quipu Systems, a Peruvian software, data and AI company. Create a NEW image informed by the supplied references, not a collage or replica photograph.
Reference roles: Image 1 quipu-2.webp establishes the real overall construction, one horizontal primary twisted cord and numerous pendant cords attached with hitches; Image 2 quipu-3.jpg establishes the anatomy of actual knots, repeated wraps, single knots and subsidiary cords; Image 3 quipus-news.webp establishes natural drape, a gently curved main cord and a fan-like distribution of pendants; Image 4 FIC_6197.webp establishes grouping and changes in cord thickness, spacing and length. These are structural references, not the final colors or backgrounds.
Primary request: A striking premium photoreal CGI studio render of a complete recognizable QUIPU reinterpreted for an ambitious modern technology company. Preserve the quipu's topology: one substantial twisted primary cord spanning across the upper third in a shallow sweeping curve, with roughly 26 to 34 separate slender braided pendant cords individually hitched along it, hanging down under gravity at variable lengths, gently fanning apart toward their ends. Include a few finer subsidiary pendant branches. Clearly legible tied knots and elongated clusters of wrapped knots at varied levels along these cords. Knots must be made of the cords themselves, tactile and structurally convincing. The entire quipu is the subject, not one giant central knot.
Art direction: ancestral information architecture meets contemporary precision. Soft braided graphite and satin silver microfilaments with selective deep teal fibers and tiny restrained cyan highlights woven ALONG a few strands and around a few actual knot contours. Around 70% graphite/silver, 30% teal. Preserve the organic delicate rope character; sophisticated, tactile, beautifully crafted, no hard cable connectors, no electrical plugs, no metal cylinders, no glowing beads, no sci-fi machinery, no torus or closed central ring.
Composition: square high-resolution image, full object centered with generous 12-15% near-black negative space on every edge, slight three-quarter perspective for depth while keeping the entire main cord and attachments visible; graceful broad upper span, descending curtain of cords, gently staggered ends. Freestanding floating museum-quality sculpture, no frame, no wall, no visible support, no people. Object fills about 76% width and 78% height.
Lighting: cinematic large softbox silver highlights, restrained turquoise rim light, subtle soft grounding shadow. Deep near-black background #080c0f seamless into every outer edge. Refined quiet lighting, no neon haze, no particles, no interface, no typography, no logo, no watermark. Make this feel like an iconic brand artifact with cultural specificity, not a stock tech abstraction.
